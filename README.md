## 👥 HR Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive **HR Analysis Dashboard built with Microsoft Power BI** to analyze employee workforce data, attrition trends, salary slabs, departments, gender, age groups, job roles, job satisfaction, and employee experience.

---

## 🎯 Project Overview

The objective of this project is to transform HR employee data into an interactive **Human Resources analytics dashboard**.

The dashboard helps answer important workforce questions:

- How many employees are in the organization?
- How many employees are active?
- What is the total attrition count and attrition rate?
- Which departments have the highest attrition?
- Which salary slabs have higher attrition?
- How does attrition differ by gender?
- Which job roles show higher attrition?
- How does job satisfaction relate to attrition?
- Which age groups contain the largest employee population?
- How does attrition vary with employee experience?

----

## 🏢 Business Problem

Raw employee records can make it difficult for HR teams to quickly identify workforce trends and attrition patterns.

This dashboard converts employee data into an interactive reporting solution that helps management:

- Monitor workforce KPIs
- Track employee attrition
- Identify high-attrition departments
- Analyze salary-related attrition patterns
- Understand workforce demographics
- Compare job roles and satisfaction
- Analyze employee experience
- Support data-driven HR decisions

----

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI Desktop**

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 📌 KPI Cards

The dashboard provides six key HR KPIs:

- 👥 Total Employee
- ✅ Active Employee
- 🚪 Attrition Count
- 📉 Attrition Rate %
- 🎂 Average Age
- 💼 Average Experience

----

### 🏢 Attrition Count by Department

A donut chart compares employee attrition across departments and helps identify areas that may require further HR investigation.

### 💰 Attrition by Salary Slab

A horizontal bar chart analyzes attrition across:

- 0–3 LPA
- 3–6 LPA
- 6–10 LPA
- 10+ LPA

### 👨‍👩‍👧 Attrition by Gender

A donut chart compares attrition between male and female employees.

### 👔 Attrition by Job Role & Job Satisfaction

A matrix analyzes attrition by job role, job satisfaction level, and total attrition, helping identify potential risk areas.

### 🎂 Age Group Distribution

A column chart displays employee distribution across:

- 18–25
- 26–35
- 36–45
- 46–55
- 55+

### 📈 Attrition Trend by Experience

An area chart analyzes attrition according to total employee experience.

### 🏢 Department-Wise Employee

A funnel-style visual compares employee counts across departments and highlights workforce distribution.

### 🔎 Department Filter

An interactive department filter allows users to analyze HR metrics for individual departments.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

Based on the dashboard view:

- **1,403 employees** are included in the analyzed dataset.
- **1,186 employees** are active.
- Total attrition is **231 employees**.
- Overall attrition rate is approximately **16.30%**.
- Average employee age is approximately **36.94 years**.
- Average experience is approximately **7.04 years**.
- The **26–35 age group** represents the largest employee segment.
- Attrition varies across departments and salary slabs.
- Job role and job satisfaction can be analyzed together to identify potential attrition risk areas.

> These insights are based on the dashboard preview and can change when filters are applied.
